Slow Down Days in the Garraf Hills

Garraf hills, Catalonia, Spain (example location) · „Mas Brisaclara“ (fictional name) · 4 days / 3 nights

For four days nobody needs anything from you. Long lunches, easy laughter, morning light over the hills.

These are fictional example offers. They show how retreats on Gatherwynd could look. You cannot book them.

Mas Brisaclara sits on a slope above the coast, twenty minutes inland, where the pines start and the noise stops. Four days with a loose rhythm: a little yoga, a lot of walking, long lunches and afternoons that belong to you.

A day here

  1. 07:30Coffee on the terrace while the light comes over the hills.
  2. 08:00Gentle yoga in the old barn, 45 minutes, for every level.
  3. 09:00Breakfast: bread from the village bakery, olive oil, tomatoes, figs when they are in season.
  4. 10:30Walk on the old path towards the sea, about an hour and a half. Or stay in the garden with a book.
  5. 14:00Long lunch at the table under the pergola.
  6. 15:30Free time: siesta, hammock, the small pool.
  7. 19:00Short walk to the viewpoint for the evening light.
  8. 20:30Dinner together. Nobody has to talk, but usually everyone does.

Programme

  1. Day 1

    Arrival from 15:00. Tea and almond biscuits, a look at your room, first dinner at the long table.

  2. Day 2

    Morning yoga, walk to the sea viewpoint, a free afternoon.

  3. Day 3

    A slow day. If you like, you cook with us in the afternoon: pa amb tomàquet and escalivada.

  4. Day 4

    Last yoga, a long breakfast, goodbye around 11:00.

Where you sleep

An old stone farmhouse with 9 rooms. The thick walls keep it cool, the beds have white cotton linen, and from some windows you see a strip of sea between the pines.

Food

Mostly vegetarian Mediterranean cooking from what the region has: bread, olive oil, vegetables from the garden, rice dishes, fruit. Vegan on request.

Small moments

  • The crickets start as soon as the evening gets warm.
  • Someone has already put the kettle on when you come down.
  • Fig leaves smell of summer on the way back from the walk.
  • Nobody asks what you do for a living until the third day.

Good to know

Group
Up to 12 people
Arrival
Train to Sitges, from there 20 minutes by car (pick up possible)
Bring
Comfortable shoes and a warm layer for the evenings

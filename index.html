About us

Placeholder Video by Nils (16:9), comes from Nils

Nils

Gatherwynd is run by Nils. For many years he was a yoga and shiatsu teacher, coach and retreat leader, and today he lives near Barcelona. His own path had its detours too, and one of them led him to ask what a place needs so that people can truly come to rest there. That experience is part of everything he selects.

There are videos of the centers that show you honestly what they look like. Whether in the Latin American jungle or somewhere in Europe.

gather + wynd = Gatherwynd

The name

Gatherwynd is a made-up word with two parts. "Gather" means to collect, to come together. "Wynd" is an old Scots word for a narrow path or lane. Together it means: the path that brings people together.

That is exactly what it is about for us. The path to a place where you can arrive. And the people you meet there.

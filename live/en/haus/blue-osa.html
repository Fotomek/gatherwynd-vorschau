Blue Osa Yoga Retreat and Spa

Playa Tamales, Puerto Jiménez, Osa Peninsula

Costa RicaRetreatPackages from USD 900

Location

On Playa Tamales near Puerto Jiménez on the Osa Peninsula. Private grounds, for registered guests only.

Getting there

From San José, according to the house: domestic flight to Puerto Jiménez (about 40 minutes), shuttle or private transfer (5 to 6 hours).

Type and program

Yoga retreat and spa with wellness packages, group retreats and yoga teacher trainings.

Accommodation

13 rooms for up to 36 guests.

Meals

Farm to table cooking: breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks are included in the packages.

Prices

Retreat packages from USD 1,440 (4 day weekend) to USD 2,300 (one week).

  • Included: accommodation, all meals, daily yoga and spa amenities. The price depends on room type and occupancy.
